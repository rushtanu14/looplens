# LoopLens

register: product

## Purpose and users
LoopLens is a browser-local practice bench for teen programmers learning why a loop behaves differently from their prediction. A student works through one small loop, predicts the accumulator, steps through the exact variable trace, changes a bound or direction, and exports evidence of the first differing iteration.

## Product contract
Support integer start/end/step values, less-than or less-than-or-equal bounds, sum/count accumulation, three purposeful challenges, custom editable loops, an explicit compared version, and a Markdown debug receipt. Never execute arbitrary code. Bound every simulation to 200 iterations and identify incomplete results. Save only configuration and prediction in the current browser; recover gracefully from corrupt or unavailable storage. Reset has deterministic behavior.

## Voice and strategy
Be precise, calm, curious and encouraging without grading the student as a person. A wrong prediction is useful evidence. Explain the concrete state transition before introducing jargon. No AI tutor claim, invented metrics, account system, backend, remote analytics or fabricated deployments.

## Success hypothesis
Seeing two traces with an exact first differing iteration lets a beginner explain an off-by-one error using values rather than guessing at syntax. Prove the product mechanics with deterministic domain tests, browser integration tests, desktop/mobile flows, and a working downloaded receipt. Educational impact remains unmeasured.
